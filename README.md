# My favorite Series: Arcane
>*"Some things are worth fighting for."*
Arcane is not just a favorite, it is also one of my comfort series. Other than because it was based on one of my favorite games, League of Legends, I took a liking of it because of its style and plot. The story explores realistic emotions and relationships, while keeping it exiting and enticing. Another thing that I love about it was the soundtracks or songs that they use on certain scenes.
